# Cinema Lives Here — brand fonts

Drop font files here (OTF, TTF, or WOFF2):

## Required

- **Nohemi** (English) — any/all weights from Light → Black
- **Panorama** (Arabic display) — Bold or equivalent
- **Rawasi** (Arabic body) — Regular or equivalent

Claude will move these into `brands/red-sea/assets/fonts/` and register them in the Remotion project once they arrive.

## If the Dropbox folder download gives you a ZIP

Just extract locally and upload the individual font files, or push the ZIP and Claude will extract.
